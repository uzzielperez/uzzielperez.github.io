Sometimes it's the small things that remind you that you're alive, that you're moving forward, that things are okay. Here are some signs of life I've been noticing lately:

**Playing football again.** There's something about running on a field, the feel of grass under your feet, the coordination of body and mind working together. It's a return to something I love, a reminder that my body can still do the things it used to do.

**Traveling and giving talks.** Being able to share ideas, to connect with people through work and passion. The movement, the conversations, the exchange of knowledge—these are signs that I'm engaged with the world again.

**Having friends.** The simple, profound gift of connection. People who care, who check in, who make you laugh. It's easy to take for granted, but it's everything.

**I have a place to stay and am eating ok.** The fundamentals. A roof over my head, food in my stomach. These aren't small things—they're the foundation that makes everything else possible.

**I have health insurance.** Security. Peace of mind. The knowledge that if something goes wrong, I'll be okay. It's a privilege, and I'm grateful for it.

**The fundamentals are in order.** When the basics are covered, you can breathe. You can think. You can plan. You can live.

These aren't dramatic victories or life-changing moments. They're quiet reassurances that life is moving forward, that things are stable, that I'm okay. And sometimes, that's enough. Sometimes, these small signs of life are everything.
